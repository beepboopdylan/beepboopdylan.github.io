In Spring 2024, I coordinated an event for the [Woodenfish Foundation](https://www.woodenfishfoundation.org/home) and [Columbia University Department of Religion](https://religion.columbia.edu/content/buddhism), where I coordinated a historic visit from [Venerable Dr. Yifa, PhD.](https://asiacenter.harvard.edu/venerable-yifa) and an international delegation of over 20 Buddhist monks and nuns to Columbia University.

The delegation came from around the world, from Thailand, Taiwan, and Malaysia. Their visit was a part of Woodenfish's Buddhist Sangha Education Study Tour, which empowers Buddhist monastics to pursue higher education. With Columbia's leading programs in Buddhist and East Asian studies programs, the visit aimed to expose the monastics, many of whom had never been to the U.S. before, nor attended higher education, to what an American university education looks like.

After connecting Venerable Dr. Yifa with Columbia faculty, I helped organize a series of meetings and dialogues with admissions officers at the [Union Theological Seminary](https://utsnyc.edu/), and with professors in the Department of Religion and Buddhist Studies. These meetings allowed the monastics to learn about the application process, curriculum, and campus life at Columbia and UTS. Financial aid and scholarship opportunities were also discussed to help make higher education more accessible and realistic for monastics.

Buddhism is rooted in the study of our reality - a scientific approach to understanding the mind and the world around us.
